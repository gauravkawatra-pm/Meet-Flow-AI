 MeetFlow AI — Meeting-to-Execution Agent

Turn meetings into action. Keep decisions connected. Prevent delivery surprises.

MeetFlow AI is an AI-powered meeting assistant that transforms meeting transcripts into structured, actionable project information. It helps project managers, product owners, and delivery teams capture decisions, assign action items, identify risks, and reduce manual follow-up.

Built for the Product Space × Wispr Flow Agentic AI Hackathon.

🎯 The Problem

Meetings generate important decisions, commitments, and action items, but these are often scattered across transcripts, notes, emails, and chat messages.

As a result:

Action items may lack clear owners or deadlines.

Important decisions can be forgotten between meetings.

Conflicting commitments can go unnoticed.

Project managers spend time manually chasing updates.

Delivery risks may only become visible when deadlines are missed.

💡 The Solution

MeetFlow AI turns meeting conversations into actionable project intelligence.

Upload a meeting transcript, analyze it with AI, and extract the information your team needs to move forward.

Key Features

📎 Transcript Upload: Upload supported transcript files directly through the web interface.

📝 Meeting Summaries: Generate concise summaries of meeting discussions.

✅ Decision Extraction: Identify decisions explicitly agreed upon during meetings.

📋 Action Items: Extract tasks, owners, deadlines, and statuses.

🔗 Dependencies: Identify relationships between tasks.

⚠️ Risk Detection: Highlight blockers, risks, and potential delivery issues.

🧠 Decision Memory — Planned: Maintain a history of decisions across meetings.

🔍 Conflict Detection — Planned: Flag potential contradictions between new discussions and previous commitments.

📅 Weekly Digest — Planned: Generate personalized summaries of outstanding actions and upcoming deadlines.

Features marked as planned are part of the product roadmap and may not yet be implemented.
